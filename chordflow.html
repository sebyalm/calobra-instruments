Workflow

generate, then make it yours.

  1. Set the key and genre. Ten scales, from major and minor to Dorian, Lydian and both pentatonics.
  2. Dial Mood and Complexity. Mood runs dark to bright. Complexity runs from plain triads to 9ths, 11ths and 13ths.
  3. Press Generate. The timeline fills with chords, each slot as wide as it lasts.
  4. Lock what you like, re-roll the rest. Pick a slot and the drawer suggests chords that fit, ranked by stars.
  5. Drag MIDI into your DAW, or let it play in sync with the host.
  6. Save it to your library. The preset menu also keeps your last 20 generations, so a good idea is never lost.

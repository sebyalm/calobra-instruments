Unmask

look under the mask.

Hit Unmask and the camera cranes up over the ring. The move's effect chain is laid out on the canvas from In to Out, each module with its own knobs. The lamp on each module is its bypass, and bypassing never clicks. Remask takes you back.

On the main view, the eight turnbuckle knobs are the move's macros, and the last one is always Mix. The four moves sit on the ring apron, In and Out trim your levels, and + Save keeps your own version.

What do I get when I buy?

Each plugin as a VST3 and as a standalone app. Lucha Effects and Move-a-Side chain also come as AU on macOS.

Is it a one-time purchase?

Yes. You buy a licence once and keep it, with free updates. No subscription.

Is Lucha Effects really free?

Yes. Enter your email and we'll send you the full plugin, with all nine luchadores and 36 moves.

Can I try the paid plugins first?

Yes. Move-a-Side chain, ChordFlow, Twig and RoboVoco each have a free, limited demo you can run in your own sessions.

Which systems does it run on?

All five run on Windows 64-bit. Lucha Effects and Move-a-Side chain also run on macOS as VST3 and AU.

How do I install a plugin?

Copy the .vst3 file into C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3, then rescan plugins in your DAW. The standalone app runs on its own, no DAW needed.

Is the bundle cheaper than buying one by one?

Yes. The bundle gets you all five for less than the four paid licences on their own.

Signal flow

four panels, left to right.

The faceplate reads the way the audio flows. Each panel switches on and off on its own.

Sidechain

  • Depth of the duck
  • Trigger: Key In or Tempo
  • Rate 1/1 to 1/16, triplet and dotted
  • Key filter: low cut, hi cut
  • Envelope: attack, release
  • Display: Spect, Scope, Delta

Link

  • Amount the duck moves the width
  • Reaction speed
  • Duck: Full, Low, Mid or High

Stereo width

  • Width from mono up
  • Bands: low, mid, high, with solo
  • Crossover: low and high split
  • Stereoize in milliseconds
  • Link bands to edit together

Output

  • Mix dry to wet
  • Gain −12 to +12 dB
  • VU meters L and R
  • Bypass
Quick start

duck a bass in under a minute.

  1. Put it on the track you want to duck, like a bass or a pad, and pick a preset. 16 come built in, like Bass under kick and Pad pump 1/4.
  2. Key it from the kick. Open your DAW's sidechain input on the plugin and pick the kick track.
  3. No kick yet? Flip Trigger to Tempo and pick a rate. It ducks in time with the host.
  4. Turn up Link and choose a band to duck. The width moves with the pump.

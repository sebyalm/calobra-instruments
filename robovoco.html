Demos played through RoboVoco, straight out of the plugin.

Four modes

one signal path, four characters.

A mode is a position on six controls inside one engine, not a separate chain. Switching is instant and silent, and all four sit within a decibel of each other.

Robot

A filterbank with hard ring modulation. The off-key partials are what make it sound like a machine.

Vocoder

A 24-band channel vocoder, tuned so the words stay clear.

Talkbox

A model of one vocal tract, re-fitted to the singer about every 5 ms.

Acid

A four-pole ladder filter with envelope and tempo-locked motion on the cutoff.

The front panel

six controls do the work.

Key and Scale sit next to a live readout of the note it hears, so the carrier lands on the right notes. Set them to your song first.

Character

How hard the transformation bites.

Formant

Moves the whole vocal tract up to an octave either way.

Clarity

Turn it up when the words aren't coming through.

Motion

Depth of the tempo-locked movement on cutoff and gate.

Smooth

Pulls back any band that rings above its neighbours.

Mix

Equal-power dry and wet.

Rhythm and presets

made for breakbeat vocals.

Patterns chop the voice in time: Offbeat, Breaks, Stutter and Tresillo, with swing. Delay and reverb duck under the voice and come back in the gaps. Route MIDI to play chords, or send your own synth into the carrier input.
